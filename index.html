Before we start

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Costs, timelines, maintenance and SEO — the things to know before starting your website project.

How much does a business website cost?+

A one-page landing site typically starts around €400. A multi-page business website with a catalog, forms and SEO setup usually lands between €700 and €1,800. You always get a fixed quote before we start.

How long does it take?+

Most business websites go live in 1–2 weeks once I have your texts, photos and logo. Landing pages are often ready in a few days; larger catalog or e-commerce projects can take 3–4 weeks. We agree on a timeline for your project.

Will it work well on phones?+

Every site I build is mobile-first and responsive. Layouts, images and forms are designed for phones first, then scaled up to tablet and desktop.

Is SEO included?+

Clean semantic HTML, titles and descriptions, structured data, fast loading and a sitemap are part of the work. The foundations are there so search engines can understand your website.

What happens after launch?+

I offer optional monthly maintenance covering content updates, technical fixes, dependency updates and performance checks, so your site keeps working while you focus on your business.

Do you work with clients outside Italy?+

Yes. I'm based in Italy and work remotely with clients anywhere. Communication happens by email or video call, and you always talk directly to the person building your site.
